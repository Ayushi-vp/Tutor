"A few scrypt hashes per second" expected at N=2^17 (≈ 0.3-0.5 s each) — unmeasured. Step 6 hits the live HIBP range API (network); response lines are "SUFFIX:COUNT", uppercase hex; may need a User-Agent header. Worked numbers checked by script: 36^8 = 2,821,109,907,456 → 128 s at 21,975.5 MH/s; 15.5 years at 5,750/s; 14,344,391 × 77 = 1.10e9 → 53.4 h; PBKDF2 600k ≈ 14,761/s; 14,601/(14,601+273,465) = 5.07%. Argon2 GPU ceiling (~10k/s, ~1,200 states in 24 GB) is a back-of-envelope bound, not a benchmark. Drill bcrypt timings (15 ms at cost 8) are an assumption stated as such. |
